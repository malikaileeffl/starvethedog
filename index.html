01 — The Meaning

There are two dogs inside you.
One is going to win.

Two dogs live inside every man and woman who follows Christ. One is the flesh — comfort, ease, lust, pride, the path of least resistance. The other is the Spirit — discipline, sacrifice, love, truth, the narrow road.

They’re at war. Every day. And the one you feed is the one that wins.

Starve The Dog is one hour of brutal physical work followed by one hour in Scripture. We starve what wars against the Spirit by feeding what feeds it. We sharpen iron. We sweat. We open the Word. We go home different than we came.

Galatians 5:16–17

“So I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the flesh desires what is contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit what is contrary to the flesh. They are in conflict with each other…”
